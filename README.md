# Hi, I'm Søren

## Apps I sell

- 📊 **[SessionWatcher](https://sessionwatcher.com/?ref=github.com)**: live limits, usage and costs for 22 AI coding tools (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI and more) in the Mac menu bar or the Windows taskbar, with an iPhone and Apple Watch app in beta. 879 paying customers
- 🐢 **[TurtleBar](https://www.turtlebar.app/?ref=github.com)**: battery time remaining in the Mac menu bar, estimated from watts rather than percent. It tells you whether you will last until 6 pm, shows what is draining the battery, and turns on Low Power Mode by itself
- ⚓ **[Moorline](https://www.moorline.app/?ref=github.com)**: native Mac customer support app for software founders, on the mailbox you already have. It remembers which customers still need a reply after you investigate, fix, refund or ship

## Free for teachers and students

- 🪑 **[UbiSit](https://ubisit.forge.apps.education.fr/ubisit-plan-de-classe-aleatoire/?ref=github.com)**: classroom seating plans for teachers. Draw the room, import the class (straight from Pronote with its browser extension), then seat students at random or by hand. Free and open source; student lists never leave the computer. 30,179 visits since February 2024
- 🎓 **[ImproveGrade](https://improvegrade.com/?ref=github.com)**: grade calculator for French university students, with official coefficients, exams, coursework and labs, and the grade to aim for in each subject

## Open source

- 🧩 **[STARCK UI](https://ui.starck.studio/?ref=github.com)**: shadcn components taken from my apps, installable with the shadcn CLI as `@starck` ([source](https://github.com/Soren-Starck/starck-ui))
- 🐦 **[SubFeed for X](https://github.com/Soren-Starck/subfeed-for-x)**: browser extension for X on the web that hides the posts you have already read and anything older than 72 hours, and cuts X down to Home, Notifications and your Following timeline
- 🔋 **[battime](https://github.com/Soren-Starck/battime)**: battery time remaining, power draw and health for Mac laptops, in the terminal
- ✏️ **[CotEditor Terminal Edition](https://github.com/Soren-Starck/CotEditor-Terminal-Edition)**: a fork of CotEditor, the lightweight macOS text editor, with an integrated terminal
- ✉️ **[Support email templates](https://github.com/Soren-Starck/support-email-templates)**: copy-ready replies for software founders, from "bug fixed" to refunds and price increases
